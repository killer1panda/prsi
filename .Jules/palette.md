## 2024-03-22 - [Vanilla HTML Micro-UX improvements]
**Learning:** For single-page vanilla HTML/JS prototypes like `frontend.html`, applying micro-UX improvements (e.g. `aria-live`, label associations, and disabled button stylings) can significantly improve basic screen reader capability and provide dynamic loading feedback without needing to add extra frameworks.
**Action:** Always ensure that simple `style` block modifications or inline attributes account for `:focus-visible`, disabled states, and ARIA labels.
